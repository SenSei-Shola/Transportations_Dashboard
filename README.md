# Transportation Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes transportation passenger data to uncover important trends in ridership, route performance, passenger demographics, bus utilization, and travel patterns.

The analysis was carried out using **Microsoft Excel**, where raw transportation data was processed, analyzed, and transformed into interactive dashboard visuals. The project provides insights that can support transportation planning, resource allocation, and operational decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze total passenger ridership across transportation routes.
- Identify the busiest and least busy transportation routes.
- Examine passenger travel patterns across different times of the day.
- Compare peak and off-peak transportation periods.
- Analyze ridership trends by month, year, and day of the week.
- Evaluate bus utilization levels.
- Analyze passenger demographics by gender and age group.
- Identify the top-performing transportation routes.
- Calculate key performance indicators to support decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🚌 Route Performance

The **East-West Express** was identified as the busiest route, with **1,322 passengers**, followed closely by the **Central Line**, with **1,271 passengers**.

Other major routes analyzed include:

- Airport Express
- University Line
- Suburban Line
- Market Line

These insights can help transportation managers identify routes with high passenger demand and allocate vehicles accordingly.

### ⏰ Time Analysis

Passenger activity was analyzed across different time periods to identify busy and less busy travel periods.

The analysis showed that the **10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** period recorded the highest ridership, with **2,119 passengers**, followed by the **7:00 AM – 10:00 AM** period, with **2,099 passengers**.

This information can help improve:

- Bus scheduling
- Driver allocation
- Fleet deployment
- Passenger waiting times

### 📅 Trend Analysis

Ridership was analyzed across:

- Months
- Years
- Days of the week

The dashboard also examines year-on-year changes and compares passenger volumes against average ridership levels.

### 🚌 Bus Utilization Analysis

Transportation buses were categorized according to utilization levels:

- **Over-Utilized**
- **Well-Utilized**
- **Under-Utilized**

This analysis helps identify potential inefficiencies in fleet allocation and provides insights into where additional buses may be required or where resources may be underused.

### 👥 Passenger Demographics

Passenger demographics were analyzed based on:

- Gender
- Age brackets
- Average passenger age

The age groups include:

- 0–19
- 20–29
- 30–39
- 40–49
- 50–59
- Other age categories

This analysis helps provide a better understanding of the transportation system's customer base.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Increase capacity on high-demand routes**, particularly the East-West Express and Central Line.
2. **Optimize bus scheduling during peak periods** to reduce overcrowding and passenger waiting times.
3. **Review under-utilized routes and buses** to improve operational efficiency.
4. **Allocate resources based on time-based demand patterns** rather than using a uniform schedule throughout the day.
5. **Use demographic insights** to improve customer-focused transportation services.
6. **Monitor year-on-year ridership trends** to support long-term transportation planning.
